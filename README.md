# Sales Performance Analysis & Business Intelligence Dashboard

**Bank Muamalat × Rakamin Academy — Business Intelligence Analyst Project-Based Internship**

This project analyzes sales transaction data from **PT Sejahtera Bersama** as part of the Bank Muamalat Business Intelligence Analyst Project-Based Internship. The analysis focuses on combining multiple relational datasets, building a master transaction table in **Google BigQuery**, and developing a sales performance dashboard in **Looker Studio**.

The goal is to identify revenue drivers, sales-volume patterns, and geographic performance to support better business decisions.

---

## Project Objectives

- Identify the **primary key** of each dataset and define relationships between tables.
- Combine Customer, Orders, Products, and Product Category data into a single master transaction table.
- Calculate **Total Sales** from order quantity and product price.
- Analyze sales and order quantity by **product category** and **customer city**.
- Identify top-performing product categories and locations.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.
